Christmas Santa Dog Bandana – Free Crochet Pattern

Dress up your furry best friend for the holidays! This Santa-inspired pet bandana features an adjustable tie-on collar, a classic black belt with a golden buckle, and fluffy trim. Designed in two sizes to fit everything from cats and small pups to large dogs.

Quick Project Overview

Skill Level
Advanced Beginner / Easy
Small (Cats & Dogs under 20 lbs)
Bandana = 6.5" H × 5.5" W | Collar = 21" L × 1" H
Medium / Large (Dogs over 20 lbs)
Bandana = 8" H × 8.5" W | Collar = 34" L × 1.5" H

Construction: The collar and triangle bandana are worked flat separately, edged with fluffy fleece trim, and sewn together with a surface-stitched belt buckle.

Materials & Tools

Pattern & Sizing Notes

  • Ch 1 at the beginning of a row does not count as a stitch.
  • Always start your first stitch in the very first stitch (the same stitch attached to the turning chain).
  • The triangle is worked from the bottom point upward and naturally curves slightly.
  • Custom Collar Sizing: Measure your pet’s neck comfortably. Add 10" (for tying ends). Chain until your foundation chain reaches that total measurement, then add 1 extra turning chain.
