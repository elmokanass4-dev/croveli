Free Pumpkin Cat Crochet Pattern

Bring a little Halloween character to your shelf with this free pumpkin cat crochet pattern. The project pairs a small black cat with a purple witch hat, an orange pumpkin, and a leafy finishing detail.

A closer look at the project

This project brings together several small components, so the instructions are arranged by piece with a separate finishing section. Keep the two cat hooks and pumpkin hook labeled as you work.

The rich purple, orange and green details are the little touches that give the finished display its autumn character.

  • A sculpted pumpkin with an eight-lobed leaf cap
  • A pointed witch hat with openings for the ears
  • Small embroidered facial details

Before you begin

US crochet terms. Finished height is approximately 12 cm using the source materials.

Use 2.00 mm for the cat, 2.20 mm for the hat and band, and 2.25 mm for the pumpkin and leaves.

The source describes wire, glued parts and small eyes as a decorative construction. Its construction choices have been retained.

Assembly & finishing

  1. Check the source attachment positions for the legs, tail, ears and head.
  2. Position the green leaf cap on the pumpkin with the reverse side outward.
  3. Pin the cat centrally on the leaf cap and pumpkin, check the balance, then sew or glue it in place.

The hat and pumpkin turn this little cat into a miniature autumn display. Let the finishing details settle into place, then give your pumpkin companion a spot on a shelf or desk.
