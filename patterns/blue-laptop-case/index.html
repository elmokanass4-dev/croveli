A note before you start

In Materials & Tools, the Yarn, Hook, and Notions fields contain overview text rather than their corresponding material details; a hook size is not specified. Confirm your materials before starting.

Pattern Details

Protect your tech in style! This chunky, textured laptop sleeve is worked holding two strands of worsted yarn together for extra cushion and durability. It’s an easy one-piece project finished with a simple fold-and-seam construction.

Quick Project Overview

Skill Level
Advanced Beginner / Easy
Finished Size
Approx. 14" wide × 9" high (when buttoned closed)
Construction
Worked flat in one piece from bottom to top, folded, and seamed with a slip-stitch border.

Materials & Tools

Yarn Tip: You will hold two strands together throughout the entire pattern. You can work from two separate skeins or wind one large skein into two equal balls.

Gauge & Measurements

  • Gauge: 7 sc × 13 rows = 4" × 4" (10 × 10 cm) in pattern stitch.
  • Gauge Swatch: Ch 27 and follow the stitch pattern. Count only single crochet stitches (not chain spaces).

How to Customize the Size

  • Width: Adjust your starting chain in multiples of 2 + 1 (fewer chains for a smaller tablet; more chains for a 15"+ laptop).
  • Height: Add or subtract repeats of Row 2 before working the final buttonhole row.
