Free Bow-Knot Handbag Crochet Pattern

Make a soft crochet handbag with alternating mesh rounds and a handle shaped into a bow. Start with an oval base, build the openwork body, and taper the straps into flared bow ends.

Before you start: The bunny charm shown in the photograph is decorative. Charm instructions are not included in this handbag pattern.

A closer look at the project

A simple mesh body keeps the fabric open and light, while shaped handles create the bow detail at the top. The cream project photograph shows the silhouette and stitch texture.

The handles are worked in rows from the bag rim. Decreases narrow each handle, and increases widen the bow ends before they taper again.

  • Oval double-crochet base
  • Alternating mesh and single-crochet rounds
  • Shaped handles with flared bow ends

Before you begin

US crochet terminology. Handle and bow instructions are worked back and forth in rows, even where the source uses an R label.

Follow the listed repeats and stitch counts; finished bag dimensions are not specified in the supplied pattern.

Let’s make it, one piece at a time.

Check off instructions to keep your place. Round ranges include every round to work; repeat the instruction for each round in the range. Rows are worked back and forth. Named actions are separate from round and row counts.

Handles and center straps

Make two identical handles. Locate the center 28 stitches on the front and back top edges; attach yarn and work across these stitches.

Flared bow ends

Continue from the 8 stitches of each center strap. Work back and forth in rows.

Assembly & finishing

  1. Continue decreasing the bow ends until tapered to 1 stitch, then fasten off and weave in the ends.
  2. Secure the bow knot with hot glue or needle and thread, as listed in the supplied materials.

Weave in the loose ends and check the attachments before using your finished bag.
