A note before you start

This guide uses UK crochet terms. The supplied text describes the shaping but does not give the exact stitch-by-stitch sequence or counts for the sides and tip. No missing stitches have been invented; check the supplied guidance before starting.

Pattern Instructions

Construction Overview

The leaf is worked in one piece in the round around a central foundation chain:

  • You begin with a foundation chain of 13 chain stitches (the unworked chains at the base form the small stem).
  • Work along the top side / upper loops of the chain stitches toward the tip.
  • At the leaf tip / end of the chain, rotate the work and continue working down along the underside / bottom loops of the chain back toward the base.
  • Fasten off.
