A closer look at the project

Granny Breeze pairs open stitchwork with a simple tote shape and sewn-on handles. The two-panel construction lets you build the front and back separately before joining the bag.

The pattern provides separate panel and side-depth instructions for Lion Brand 24/7 Cotton and 2 mm cotton rope. Choose one version and follow that path throughout.

  • Two matching granny-style panels
  • Cotton-yarn and cotton-rope versions
  • Sewn-on handles

Before you begin

Choose a yarn version before starting: 24/7 Cotton panels finish at round 13; 2 mm cotton-rope panels finish at round 8.

The source labels the three-sided edging as rounds, although the work is turned. The labels below identify them as edging passes so they are not confused with complete panel rounds.

The stated finished size is 38 × 30 cm without straps. Yarn choice and tension can change the result.

Assembly & finishing

  2. Sew the handles in place and secure the loose ends.
