A note before you start

The supplied PDF jumps from head round 7 to round 26: rounds 8–25 are absent. The supplied instructions and stitch counts are preserved without inventing the missing rounds. Read this limitation before starting. Pattern credit: Priscila Santos (@petitgatao.atelie).

A little queen, rich in detail

Pale flowing hair, a gold-and-silver crown, and a black cape give this Queen Rhaenyra amigurumi her character. The red collar and tiny chest embellishments add the finishing touches to a doll that stands approximately 15 cm tall.

This intermediate project combines shaped crochet pieces, hair strands, facial embroidery, and metallic accents. Take it one component at a time, using the reference photos alongside the instructions.

How the project comes together

Start with the head and hair, then make the ears, arms, feet, and legs. Continue with the body and skirt before adding the collar, cape, and crown. The complete supplied pattern follows below, with materials, US stitch abbreviations, and progress checkboxes to help you keep your place.
