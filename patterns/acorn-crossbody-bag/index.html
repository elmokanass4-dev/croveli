Free Acorn Crossbody Bag & Charm Crochet Pattern

Crochet a brown-and-cream crossbody bag with a ribbed lower body, a scalloped border, and a small stuffed acorn charm. The made-to-length strap clips onto D-rings for an everyday carry.

A closer look at the project

Two textures give this bag its character: front-post ribbing below and a soft cream upper section with scalloped edging. The detachable strap and miniature acorn finish the woodland look.

Work the oval bag first, then the strap and the two small charm pieces. The supplied pattern leaves the crossbody strap length to you.

  • Front-post ribbing and scalloped edging
  • Clip-on crossbody strap
  • Matching stuffed acorn charm

Before you begin

US crochet terminology. Read both round-15 instructions before starting the cream section: the scallop edging and main body use different loops of round 14.

Strap length and finished bag dimensions are not specified; work the strap to your desired crossbody length.

Assembly & finishing

  1. Attach the lobster clasps to both ends of the strap.
  2. Sew the D-rings to the top inside edges of the bag.
  3. Fasten off the acorn cap with a long tail for assembly.

Weave in the loose ends and check the attachments before using your finished bag.
