Free Christmas Green Man Crochet Pattern

A closer look at the project

This festive plush character gets its personality from a shaped muzzle, long-pile eyebrows and cheeks, and a red outfit finished with soft cream trim.

Work the head, mouth, feet, legs, thumbs, and arms first. The legs and arms are attached while crocheting the body; the separate cap and facial finishing complete the character. Read the joining rounds alongside the supplied reference pictures before starting.

  • 29 cm character in the source yarn and hook
  • Legs and arms joined into the body as you crochet
  • Sculpted face with textured hair, cheeks, eyebrows, and coat trim

Before you begin

US crochet terms. Work in continuous spirals through both loops unless otherwise instructed. Do not join, turn, or chain at the beginning of a round unless directed.

Stuff to medium density to hold the shape while allowing facial sculpting. Complete stuffing as the work progresses.

The source recommends X-stitch single crochet to reduce color shifting. Reposition the round marker with shift stitches where indicated; the source’s example shift counts are not a fixed requirement.

The finished height depends on the specified yarn and hook. Substituting yarn or changing hook size changes the result.

The source places the eye stems 9 stitches apart at round 12. Its printed round-placement wording repeats “12”; no different second round has been assumed here.

The source includes QR/video references. The construction and facial point diagrams are included below as visual references; no video links have been guessed from OCR.

Let’s make it, one piece at a time.

Check off instructions to keep your place. Round ranges include every round to work; repeat the instruction for each round in the range. Rows are worked back and forth. Named actions are separate from round and row counts.

Head

Use green Himalaya Dolphin Baby 80371.

Mouth / lip

Make a pink inner disc and a green outer disc.

Feet — make 2

Use red Himalaya Dolphin Baby 80318.

Legs — make 2

Start with red 80318. Mark 8 points in a circle on each foot as shown in the reference.

Thumbs — make 2

Use green Himalaya Dolphin Baby 80371.

Arms — make 2

Start with green 80371; join a thumb to each arm in rounds 6 and 7.

Body and limb joins

Start with green 80371. Check the reference pictures carefully for the multi-round leg and arm joins.

Santa cap

Use red Himalaya Dolphin Baby 80318.

Face sculpting and finishing

Use the lettered reference diagram with the sculpting steps. These are finishing steps, not crochet rounds.

Assembly & finishing

  1. Complete the sculpted expression and facial details using the diagram and reference photographs.
  2. Sew the head to the body, stuffing the neck so the head does not tilt.
  3. Crochet the collar under the head with sc in milk-colored Etrofil Rabbit 70111. The separate Santa cap is completed in its own section.

Weave in the ends and check all sewn attachments before displaying your finished project.
