Free Long-Eared Bunny Crochet Pattern

Make a soft, long-eared bunny with this free amigurumi crochet pattern. Work the chenille legs and arms first, join them into the body, then shape the head and add the embroidered expression.

A closer look at the project

This is a piece-by-piece amigurumi project with the legs and arms crocheted into the body. The sequence below keeps each component in its own section, so you can check your progress without losing your place.

The supplied styled photograph shows a jacket. Clothing instructions are not included in this bunny pattern.

  • Plush chenille texture with long, folded ears
  • Legs and arms joined as the body is worked
  • Eye sculpting and embroidery for the finishing details

Before you begin

US crochet terminology. The source groups repeated rounds into numbered steps; the printed step labels and repeat counts are retained below.

Stuff the pieces gradually where directed. Leave the ears unstuffed.

Keep the eye-placement and sculpting references beside you before fastening off the head.

Let’s make it, one piece at a time.

Check off instructions to keep your place. Labels and stitch counts follow the supplied pattern; repeated rounds stay grouped where indicated.

Legs · make two

Start at the oval sole and stuff gradually as you work.

Work 4 shift sc. Fold the top edge flat and crochet through both edges: 1 dec, 5 sc, 1 dec. (7) Place the decreases at the sides. Fasten off and hide the yarn inside. Make the second leg identically.

Arms · make two

Stuff the lower part firmly and the upper part lightly.

Work 5 shift sc. Fold flat and crochet across both edges: 1 dec, 3 sc, 1 dec. (5) Fasten off and weave in the end.

The little tail

Sl st and cut the yarn, leaving a tail for sewing. Leave unstuffed or stuff lightly.

Body & joined limbs

The legs are attached over two consecutive rounds. Stuff gradually as the body takes shape.

Stuff the body firmly. Sl st, cut the yarn, and secure.

Long ears · make two

Use the main plush color. Do not stuff.

Flatten the opening and crochet 9 sc across both edges. Fold the base in half lengthwise once more and crochet 4 sc through both layers; the ninth stitch sits along the fold. Fasten off and hide the yarn inside.

Head & eye placement

Stuff firmly, close the remaining hole, fasten off, and weave in the tail.

Facial sculpting & embroidery

Use semi-cotton yarn matching the body, such as YarnArt Jeans, and follow the numbered reference points.

Assembly & finishing

  1. Sew the head to the body with sturdy matching sewing thread across 3 rounds of stitches. Stitch around 2–3 times so the head is firm.
  2. Sew the tail centrally on the lower back.
  3. Sew the ears to the back of the head, roughly level with the eyebrows and about 4–5 rounds behind them. Sew each base flat in a circle.

Set the finished bunny in front of you and take a moment to adjust the ears and expression. The smallest changes in placement give this long-eared companion its personality.
