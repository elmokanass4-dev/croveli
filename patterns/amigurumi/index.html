For a plush texture, explore the long-eared bunny: the legs and arms are joined into the body as it is worked, and the face uses sculpting and embroidery. Its styled jacket is shown in the photograph; jacket instructions are not included.

For smaller cotton-thread details, look through the calico kitten’s color changes and shaping photographs. For loop-specific stitches and clothing components, explore Luna’s sleeves, skirt, hat, and cloak. Read each pattern’s notes before choosing your project.
