A closer look at the project

A small red hat, soft cream stitches, and a little heart button give the Sweet Child ornament its festive character. This is the design shown in the main photograph.

The supplied collection also includes a front-loop Santa pendant and a separate mini Santa keychain. Choose one version and follow its labeled sections; their round numbers restart for each component.

  • Three distinct Christmas designs on one page
  • Sweet Child ornament with a scalloped hem and embroidered face
  • Separate pendant and keychain instructions, with their own components

Before you begin

US crochet terms. The 11 cm finished size and the source’s Easy rating belong to Sweet Child only; the other two versions have no listed difficulty or size.

Sweet Child uses embroidered eyes. The keychain version specifies safety eyes; these are separate finishing methods for separate designs.

The pendant instructions describe a one-piece head/hat with a looped beard. Its styled source photograph also shows a full-bodied Santa; additional limbs and clothing are not described in the pendant instructions.

Repeated instruction blocks in the source are shown once here. The source note beside pendant round 14 is retained below.

Let’s make it, one piece at a time.

Check off instructions to keep your place. Round ranges include every round to work; repeat the instruction for each round in the range. Rows are worked back and forth. Named actions are separate from round and row counts.

Sweet Child: body

Begin with pastel pink 382, then change to off-white 062.

Sweet Child: head

Use pastel pink 382.

Sweet Child: arms — make 2

Start with red 056; only the red hand portions are stuffed.

Sweet Child: hat

Use red 056.

Sweet Child: finishing

Finish this version before moving to another design.

Mini pendant: main body

Use 4-ply yarn and a 2.0 mm hook. This is a separate design; restart round numbering.

Mini pendant: looped beard

Hold the body with the red/hat end pointing toward you.

Mini keychain: head

Use skin-tone yarn. This is the third design, with separate head, body, hat, and beard components.

Mini keychain: legs and body

Make two legs, then join them.

Mini keychain: hat

Use red yarn, then change to white for the brim.

Mini keychain: pom-pom and beard

Work each white component separately.

Assembly & finishing

  1. Choose one of the three designs and keep its components together.
  2. Sweet Child assembly is detailed in its own finishing section.
  3. For the mini keychain, use the body’s long tail to sew it to the head; attach the pom-pom and beard as directed in their component instructions.

Weave in the ends and check all sewn attachments before displaying your finished project.
