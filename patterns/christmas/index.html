Choose your festive crochet project

For a small decoration, explore the Santa collection. Sweet Child is listed at 11 cm with a 2.0 mm hook; the pendant and keychain are separate designs with their own instructions. Choose your version before collecting its materials.

For a larger plush character, the Green Man uses a 3.75 mm hook and is listed at 29 cm. Its limbs are crocheted into the body, and the face includes sculpting and long-pile yarn details. Review the joining diagrams before starting.
