Free Witch Cat Crochet Pattern: Luna

Make Luna, a grey witch cat with a pointed hat, wavy skirt, and plum cloak, with this free crochet pattern. This intermediate amigurumi project brings together shaped cat pieces, small clothing details, and facial embroidery.

A closer look at the project

Make the head, ears and arms before moving on to the joined body. The cuffs, skirt and hat brim are worked into reserved front loops, giving the outfit its layered finish.

A separate cloak and optional shaped tail complete the character. Keep the finished pieces together until you are ready to pin and sew the final arrangement.

  • An intermediate project in US crochet terminology
  • Layered sleeve cuffs, ruffled skirt and hat brim
  • A removable-looking cloak tied with crocheted strands

Before you begin

The source specifies intermediate skill level and American terminology.

Work in continuous rounds unless the instructions say otherwise. Luna measures approximately 13.5 cm with the listed materials.

The sleeve cuff uses light purple in the written instructions. The source’s accompanying caption mentions red; the written color direction is retained here.

Let’s make it, one piece at a time.

Check off instructions to keep your place. Labels and stitch counts follow the supplied pattern; repeated rounds stay grouped where indicated.

Head

Dark grey yarn · continuous rounds.

Fasten off with a long tail. Weave through the front loops and pull tightly to close the hole; weave in loose ends.

Ears · make two

Dark grey yarn · do not stuff.

Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.

Arms & sleeve cuffs · make two

Start with dark grey yarn. The cuffs use light purple.

Fasten off and weave in loose ends.

Body & joined arms

Start in dark grey, then change to light purple.

Stuff the body, fasten off and leave a long sewing tail.

The wavy skirt

Light purple yarn. With the body base facing away from you, join into a front loop of round 13.

Fasten off, weave in loose ends and shape the skirt into waves.

Feet & pink pads · make two

Dark grey feet with vintage pink pads.

The little cloak

Dark purple yarn · work in rows. Ch 25 and begin in the second chain from the hook.

The curved tail

Dark grey yarn · do not stuff.

Fasten off with a long sewing tail. Optionally insert wire and shape it into an S, as described in the source.

The pointed hat

Start in light purple and work in rounds.

Fasten off and weave in loose ends.

Assembly & finishing

  1. Embroider black whiskers on either side of the head between rounds 14 and 17, 2 stitches from the eyes.
  2. With black DMC thread, embroider the nose between the eyes at rounds 15–16. Add vintage-pink cheek lines at rounds 16–17, 1 stitch from the eyes.
  3. Sew the ears to the head between rounds 4 and 10, then sew the head to the body.
  4. Sew the pink pads to the feet between rounds 5 and 7. Embroider three evenly spaced pink toe lines between rounds 1 and 3.
  5. Sew the feet to the front of the body between rounds 7 and 13, 4 stitches apart.
  6. Sew the tail at the back between rounds 5 and 7. Put on the cloak and hat, then weave in remaining ends.

With her soft colors and layered outfit, Luna works beautifully as a small seasonal display. Finish by arranging the cloak and hat, then check that each sewn detail sits just where you want it.
