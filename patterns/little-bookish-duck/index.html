How to start a magic ring

Make an adjustable yarn loop, work the required stitches into the loop, then gently pull the tail to close the center.

How to work an increase

Work two single crochet stitches into the same stitch to add one stitch to your round.

How to finish neatly

Cut the yarn leaving a generous tail, pull through the last loop, and weave the tail securely through the back of your stitches.

Finishing touches

  1. Lay out your pieces and check their placement.
  2. Secure each piece with a tapestry needle and matching yarn.
  3. Weave in the remaining ends and gently shape your project.

Helpful little tips

Keep your tension comfortable

If the stitches feel difficult to work into, loosen your grip or try a slightly larger hook. Consistent, relaxed stitches make the process more enjoyable.

Make it a color story

Choose two or three shades you enjoy together. A neutral base with a little pop of color is a lovely starting point.

Give yourself time

Take a break when you need one, and mark your last completed round so it is easy to return to your project.
