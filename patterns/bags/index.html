Choose your next crochet bag

The Acorn Crossbody Bag combines front-post texture, scalloped trim, and a miniature acorn. Its strap is worked to your chosen length and attached with clasps.

The Bow-Knot Handbag uses a mesh body and shaped crochet handles. Granny Breeze uses two panels and offers separate cotton-yarn and cotton-rope construction paths.
