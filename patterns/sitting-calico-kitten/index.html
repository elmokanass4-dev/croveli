AMIGURUMI · FREE CROCHET PATTERN

Free Calico Cat Crochet Pattern

This free calico cat crochet pattern creates a sitting kitten with a patchwork coat and a tiny collar. Follow the color changes for the head and body, then use the photo sequence to shape and embroider the face.

A closer look at the project

The calico markings are worked directly into the rounds. Keep the color key close and follow the changes in order: MY means mud yellow, W means white, and BL means black.

The source uses an internal neck socket and a wire-supported neck stem to create the pose. Its construction photographs are placed beside the relevant stages below.

  • Three-color calico markings worked into the stitches
  • A shaped neck structure for the sitting pose
  • Three collar finishes included in the source

Before you begin

US crochet terminology. The 12 cm size is based on the source’s 1 mm cotton and 1.5 mm hook.

Color changes are written explicitly as MY, W and BL. Keep the yarn changes in the printed order.

The source includes wire, a bell and attached facial details. Treat those construction choices as part of the supplied design; no child-safety claim is made.

Let’s make it, one piece at a time.

Check off instructions to keep your place. Round ranges include every round to work; repeat the instruction for each round in the range. Rows are worked back and forth. Named actions are separate from round and row counts.

Head & internal neck socket

Begin in white. The first section forms the internal neck socket before the head expands.

Cut and fasten off. Weave through the front loops to close the hole.

Face shaping & embroidery

Ears · make two

Make one mud-yellow ear and one black ear.

Cut the yarn, leaving a long sewing tail. Flatten the base and sew its two sides together. Pin and sew the ears between R3 and R8, counting down from the top of the head.

Front legs · make two

Use white yarn. Do not stuff these pieces.

Cut the yarn and fasten off.

Body & joined front legs

Begin in white and stuff as you go. Keep the front legs ready for R18.

Cut the yarn and fasten off.

Back legs · make two

Stuff lightly. Leave a long tail for sewing. Sew onto R7 and R8 of the body, leaving 5–6 stitches between the back legs. Sew the inner sides of the front legs together, following the source photos.

Patchwork tail

Leave a long sewing tail and attach to R8–R9 at the back of the body.

Choose a collar finish

Assembly & finishing

  1. Check the source eye placement, ear positioning, and seated leg arrangement before securing all parts.
  2. Attach the back legs and tail at the round positions given above.
  3. Fit the chosen collar and weave in remaining ends. Preserve the designer credit when sharing finished work.

The calico colors do much of the storytelling here. Finish by checking the face and sitting posture together, then settle your little kitten somewhere its expression can be enjoyed.
